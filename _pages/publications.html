Funded Projects

  • Mapping Airport Physical Expansion Potential (Nationwide) — Airport Cooperative Research Program (ACRP) Graduate Research Award, Student PI, 2025–2026. Early-stage screening framework integrating NLCD, PAD-US, hazard layers, and zoning to assess feasible airport expansion envelopes and constraints.
  • Foundational Models for Early-Phase Clinical Trials — The Data Mine corporate collaboration (Johnson & Johnson / Celgene / Novacea / Sanofi), Data Science Lead/Contributor, 2024–2025. Pipeline design, model evaluation, and reproducible analytics for trial data (methods transfer and governance focus).
  • Traffic & Mobility Analytics — The Data Mine / FHWA TMAS collaboration, Data Science Contributor, 2024–2025. Cleaning, feature engineering, and exploratory modeling of large-scale traffic datasets to support transportation analytics.
